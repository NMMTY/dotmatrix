A single collapsible section inside [Accordion](/components/overlays/accordion) — its `value`
must be unique among its siblings, since that's what the accordion's open state tracks.

<Playground name="AccordionItem" />

## Examples

`title` is the always-visible header row; `children` is the collapsible content underneath it —
disable an item (a section that's temporarily unavailable) without removing it from the list:

<CodeBlock
  codes={[
    {
      language: "tsx",
      code: '<AccordionItem value="advanced" title="Advanced settings" disabled={!isPro}>\n  ...\n</AccordionItem>',
    },
  ]}
  preview={
    <Accordion defaultValue="basic">
      <AccordionItem value="basic" title="Basic settings">
        Standard options everyone can use.
      </AccordionItem>
      <AccordionItem value="advanced" title="Advanced settings" disabled>
        Available on the Pro plan.
      </AccordionItem>
    </Accordion>
  }
/>
