A compact glanceable card: a title, one big figure with a caption beside it, and a footnote under a
hairline rule. Weather, a single KPI, a queue depth. For several related figures use a `Grid` of
[Stat](/components/data-display/stat)s instead.

<Playground name="WidgetCard" />

## Examples

Pass `onOpen` (and `openLabel`, its accessible name) to turn the title into a button with a trailing
arrow:

  codes={[
    {
      language: "tsx",
      code: '<WidgetCard\n  title="Toronto"\n  value="5°"\n  caption="overcast clouds"\n  footer="Feels like 1°."\n  onOpen={() => {}}\n  openLabel="Open Toronto forecast"\n/>',
    },
  ]}
